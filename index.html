Welcome to El Roble Academy

For years, El Roble Academy has stood as a beacon of academic brilliance and moral integrity. We believe in providing an environment where intellectual curiosity meets character development.

Our holistic approach ensures that every student is equipped not only with academic knowledge but also with the leadership skills required to thrive in an ever-changing world.

Our Mission

To foster an inspiring educational ecosystem that empowers students to discover their passion and lead with empathy and integrity.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally as a pioneering institution that transforms brilliant minds into visionary global leaders.

Core Values

Integrity, resilience, collaboration, innovation, and dedication to personal and collective growth.

Our History 🌳

A Story Written by Generations

It all started with an idea: to create a place where every generation could leave its own mark. Throughout the years, El Roble Academy has welcomed countless students, each with a different story.

2000s

The Beginning

El Roble began building the foundations of what would become a school full of memories and generations.

2010s

Growing Together

New generations arrived, creating traditions, friendships, and moments that became part of El Roble's identity.

2020s

A New Chapter

The school continued evolving, embracing new ways of learning and adapting to a more connected world.

Our Story Continues

Every graduating class adds a new page. Every student leaves a small part of themselves behind and takes memories that cannot be found in any textbook.

Because the history of El Roble is not only about the years that have passed, but about all the people who have been part of it.

Our Campus

More Than a Place to Learn 🌳

Located in the south-central area of Tegucigalpa, Honduras, El Roble Academy is a place where school life goes beyond the classroom.

📍 Location

Our campus is located next to Residencial El Portillo and across from Residencial Concepción.

📚 Learning Spaces

The campus provides spaces where students can learn, work on projects, share ideas, and develop their skills.

🎭 Student Life

School life includes cultural activities, civic events, extracurricular activities, celebrations, and special events.

🏆 A Place Full of Memories

From the first day of school to graduation, the campus becomes part of every student's story.

🌎 Bilingual Education

El Roble Academy has a strong focus on bilingual education, giving students opportunities to develop communication skills in Spanish and English.
